Cards & accounts

Credit card outstanding is debt. Debit and TNG balances are money you have. A repayment decreases both the chosen credit card outstanding and the selected source account balance.

How it works

Outstanding = opening balance + purchases − repayments.

Record an expense under a card to increase its balance. Record a repayment to reduce the balance, without counting it as new spending.

Settings & backup

Backup your records

Export everything (including accounts and repayment settings) to a JSON file and save it in iCloud Drive or Files. Importing a backup replaces the records currently on this device.

Privacy

Stored only in this browser

No account, no bank linking, no income information, and no server. Clearing Safari website data or switching phones can erase your records unless you have a backup. To use the app across devices, you must export and restore a backup manually.

For reliable iPhone use, host this single HTML page on HTTPS (for example, GitHub Pages), then in Safari choose Share → Add to Home Screen. Keep the same URL; your records belong to that website's browser storage.
